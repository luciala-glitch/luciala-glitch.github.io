An explorer for long-running electrolysis experiments. Survey hundreds of runs, open one, zoom into its telemetry, and compare what changed before the outcome.

SYNTHETIC PUBLIC EDITION Runs, lots, and stands are fictional, and every signal is a normalized index. The failure criteria are applied internally and not shown. This build has no company code, raw data, or live connection.

ABOUT THIS BUILD

This public interface was written fresh for the portfolio. It takes the structure of the team's experiment dashboard (dataset review, telemetry zoom, milestone checks, event-order analysis) and runs it on generated data. Operational failure criteria, company datasets, source code, credentials, and infrastructure are excluded. Event order does not establish causation, and faster review is a design goal, not a measured outcome.
